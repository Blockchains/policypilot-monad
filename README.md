## v3: one mobile-native app ("Love agents. Love control. PolicyPilot.")

https://blockchains.github.io/policypilot-monad/ is now a single Next.js PWA (installable, safe-area aware, bottom tab bar on phones and tablets). Tabs:
- **Launch**: a product bar jumps to Dispatch, Prime, Spread, Clip, or custom rules, and two shortcuts open your vaults or the live feed. Under that, a three-tap console. Pick a live preset (stake, best-price swap, or pay yourself), pick one of your vaults or create that preset in one transaction, then dispatch one real action. New (v4) vaults have no cap or budget, so only the balance is checked before anything is sent; on older vaults the owner can raise the per-action cap and budget up to that vault’s on-chain cap. The owner can deposit MON from a balancer or deployer wallet. The request id is generated for you. Payments, deposits, and withdrawals use a fixed gas limit, because Monad charges the limit rather than the gas used. New blocks arrive over a WebSocket when the public endpoint connects, and the page keeps polling if it does not. Under that, Prime is two choices on one pool: Capital Protected or Max Alpha. Protected is paid 30% of each profit and loses MON only after alpha is wiped out. Alpha takes the first loss and the other 70%. A hop can move at most 5% of the pool, an unprofitable hop reverts, and a 1.5% drop from the high watermark halts new hops. The 10% fee applies only to gains above that watermark. Opening one is a single transaction, capped at 10 MON. There is no promised rate. Under that, Spread quotes Uniswap v3 at 0.30% and 0.05%, PancakeSwap v3 at 0.05%, and Kuru from this tab when a new block arrives. Kuru is a price only. A round trip settles across those v3 pools when the pool's MON increases, in a separate pool you open yourself, capped at 10 MON. Rough gas is shown beside the quote and does not block a gain. It reverts unless the pool’s MON increases. The desk keeps a pool only when its runtime bytecode is the current spread contract. It fills the size, up to the 10 MON cap, that returns the most MON, and a larger size is used when that size returns more. One signature sends it. 10% of that profit pays the wallet that submitted it, 10% goes to the wallet that opened the pool, and the rest stays. The caller pays gas, including when the transaction reverts. There is no promised rate. Clip calls the Uniswap v3 and PancakeSwap v3 pools directly, searches sizes up to 10 MON, and the team runner sends at most 20 clips per round while the quote shows a gain. Rounds stay 10 minutes apart. Gas is paid on every send and can be larger than the gain. The signing wallet keeps 5 MON. There is no promised rate. Custom agent keys and external agents are under “Custom agent” (no limit sliders: v4 vaults have no limits).
- **Activity**: a live feed of real transactions (the mainnet showcase vault plus your own), with allowed/blocked filters, plain-English revert reasons, a spend chart, and quick "run your agent" / "😈 break it" actions.
- **Agents**: your vaults, read from every factory: spent so far, a kill-switch toggle, deposit/withdraw, pull everything back, withdraw tokens, and **Remove limits** on older v1-v3 vaults.
- **Services**: the top Monad services, each either live or honestly marked "coming soon".
- **Policy**: the original PolicyPilot dashboard (testnet).

Wallets: EIP-6963 multi-wallet discovery (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Backpack, OKX, Coinbase… whatever is injected), a legacy `window.ethereum` fallback, and official deep links that open the app in a mobile wallet's browser (MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase, OKX, Backpack, Trust). **WalletConnect is not included**: it needs a Reown project ID, which means a third-party signup.

## Agent vaults: live on Monad mainnet (alpha), no spending limits

> **Alpha · unaudited contract · only deposit what you're happy to lose.** Since 18 Oct 2026 new vaults have **no spending limits and no allowlists**: no vault cap, no per-payment / per-tx cap, no budget, no token approval cap, no payee allowlist and no protocol/call allowlist. **The agent can move all of the vault's funds**, to any address or contract. You stay the owner: deposit any amount, withdraw MON and tokens, pause, `emergencyWithdrawAll`, and replace the agent key at any time.

To drop those, withdraw and open a v4 vault.

## Envio HyperIndex indexer (optional data source)
`indexer/` is an Envio HyperIndex project that indexes every PolicyPilot event on Monad testnet (PerRequestLimitSet, BudgetSet, RecipientSet, Executed, PausedSet) into `PolicyState`, `Execution`, `Recipient` and an append-only `PolicyEvent` audit log, served over GraphQL.
The viewer reads state, execution history and the audit log from it when opened with `?indexer=<graphql url>` (or `indexerUrl` in `frontend/deployment.json`), and falls back to direct RPC otherwise or if the indexer is unreachable.
- Verified against the live contract: all 8 events indexed; indexer values match direct `cast call` reads (budget 250, limit 100, totalSpent 200 mUSD, paused false). Evidence: [`docs/envio-indexer-check.txt`](docs/envio-indexer-check.txt). Video (45 s): [envio-demo.mp4](https://blockchains.github.io/policypilot-monad/envio-demo.mp4).

### Running the showcase agents without the box (GitHub Actions)

`.github/workflows/showcase-agents.yml` runs near-continuously with no external trigger: a job starts every hour (plus `workflow_dispatch`), and each job loops **one full round over all team vaults every ~10 minutes for ~5h40m** (`runner/showcase-loop.sh`). The `showcase-agents` concurrency group queues the next job without cancelling the running one, so exactly one loop is active at a time. After every round the ledger (`runner/ledger/showcase-143.jsonl`) and the snapshot (`web/public/showcase-agents-143.json`) are committed with `[skip ci]` (`runner/showcase-commit.sh`): the job rebases onto the remote, the ledger is union-merged (append-only, `.gitattributes`), and on a snapshot conflict the freshly generated file wins. The site reads the snapshot from raw.githubusercontent.com and falls back to the copy deployed with Pages.

- **Every vault, every round:** `node runner/showcase-agents.mjs run` iterates all personas in `runner/showcase-config-143.json`, each in its own try/catch, so one failure never blocks the others. A persona is skipped only when it is genuinely stuck (no agent-key secret, no vault yet, key is not the vault's executor, vault paused and its agent cannot unpause it (ShowcaseAgentVaults: the agent unpauses it first), agent gas < 0.08 MON, vault balance / budget exhausted) and the reason is written to the ledger once per reason (`status: "skipped"`) and shown on the site. Policy decisions are left to the vault on-chain.
- **Vault resolution:** before each round the runner checks on-chain that each key is the `executor()` of its configured vault; if not, it looks the vault up by executor across the v2 and legacy factories (the committed config can lag behind a migration).
- **Personas:** DCA bot, Staker, Payroll, Rogue agent, DCA bot (v1 vault), plus **Kuru market maker** (sells MON→USDC and buys MON back with USDC on Kuru's order book, approval capped by the vault), **Wrap/unwrap rebalancer** (keeps a 50/50 MON/WMON split), **Tipper** (tips a dust amount to team addresses only) and **Policy tester** (wraps a dust amount, then twice that, both allowed now that caps are lifted, replays a requestId, which still reverts, and unwraps). The **Rogue agent** used to try out-of-policy actions; with caps lifted those would succeed, so it now only makes harmless moves: dust-sized payments to team addresses (the treasury or another team agent) and an owner-only withdraw attempt that still reverts. Each spending persona sends the smallest of the runner dust size, the vault's MON balance and (on older vaults) the cap/budget left. The four new personas are `pending` until the owner creates their vaults and adds `AGENT_KEY_MM`, `AGENT_KEY_REBALANCER`, `AGENT_KEY_TIPPER`, `AGENT_KEY_TESTER`.
- **Keys:** only the agents' executor keys, stored as encrypted secrets `AGENT_KEY_<ID>`. Set them with `runner/set-actions-secrets.sh`, which needs `gh auth login`. The deployer/owner key is never used in CI, so CI cannot withdraw, change policy or unpause a vault. Recovery (box mode) uses `withdraw(owner, balance)`, never `emergencyWithdrawAll` (which would pause the vault).
- **PolicyPilot showcase agents (operated by the team): low-burn mode.** ⚠️ Alpha / unaudited: these contracts and jobs run the team's own demo money only. Rounds stay 10 minutes apart, but each round sends **one persona tx** (`PERSONAS_PER_ROUND`, default 1), chosen least-recently-active first, so all nine vaults act about 16 times a day. Clips run **one clip per hour** (`CLIPS_PER_ROUND` default 1, `CLIP_EVERY_MIN` default 60; the hard cap stays 20 per round) at dust size (`CLIP_MAX_MON` 0.01; the clip pool is a team contract and its MON returns to the agents at the daily pool exit). Gas limits are estimate + 5% (`GAS_BUFFER`) because Monad charges the full limit. Team vaults sit at **1 MON**, active agent gas wallets at **3 MON** (about 2 days of their rotated usage; refilled below 1), idle "3" agents at 0.5. ### Vault picker in the app (Agents tab)

Lists the connected wallet's own vaults from the current factory and the older ones, and the team vaults from the snapshot. For your own vaults the buttons (wrap, unwrap, Kuru swap, Uniswap / PancakeSwap swap, pay an address) send **real transactions through the vault's policy** when this browser can sign for the agent (your wallet is the executor, or the agent key is stored in this browser); otherwise they simulate, and the owner can make the connected wallet the agent in one signature (`setExecutor`). On v4 vaults any address can be paid; on an older vault, paying an address that isn't an approved payee asks the owner to allowlist it first (one extra signature). Team vaults are read-only / simulate: the agent keys never enter a browser. The **one-click starter vault** creates a v4 vault with your wallet as the agent and deposits the amount you type (any amount, 0 is fine) in a single factory transaction (`createVault` is payable; gas = estimate + 10%). It has no spending limits or allowlists: the agent can move all of the vault's funds.

### Super vaults (Agents tab, mainnet)

Three curated templates, each created and funded (any amount) in one factory transaction with your wallet as the agent, then driven by a **playbook**. They have no spending limits or allowlists: the agent can move all of the vault's funds (real transactions; a failed step stops the run and says why, it does not keep retrying):

- **Yield** — splits a deposit across Kintsu (sMON), aPriori (aprMON) and Magma (gMON). Receipt tokens stay in the vault.
- **Best execution** — quotes Uniswap v3, PancakeSwap v3 and Kuru for the same MON→USDC size and routes to the best output; the app sets minOut = best quote − 3% on each swap. The same quote panel is on the manual “Best-price swap” button whenever a vault has two or more of those venues.
- **Payments** — pays any address, any amount: no payee allowlist and no per-payment cap. The playbook pays 0.5 MON to each payee you list.

### Vault report and cash-out

Every vault (yours, and the team vaults as a read-only view) opens a portfolio: a donut and allocation bar of what is inside, each asset’s amount and estimated MON value, and two sparklines (MON/USDC, and the vault’s estimated value). Prices are quoted on-chain from Uniswap v3, PancakeSwap v3 and Kuru for 0.1 MON and scaled to 1 MON — there is no paid price API. The lines are sampled every 20 seconds while the page is open and stored in this browser only. aprMON and gMON are valued with `convertToAssets`; sMON has no exchange-rate function on its contract, so it is shown 1:1 and labeled as such. A “how this vault works” strip says plainly whether the vault has limits (v4: none) and lists the playbook.

**Cash out / close** (owner only, real transactions, stops at the first failure):

1. If the vault holds USDC, this browser can sign as the agent, a Uniswap or PancakeSwap call is allowed (always on v4; on older vaults the rule and USDC approval cap must allow it) — swap USDC → WMON on the better quote (minOut = quote − 3%). Otherwise USDC is returned as USDC.
2. If the vault holds WMON and the wrap rule is on — unwrap it to MON inside the vault. Otherwise WMON is returned as WMON.
3. `withdrawToken` every remaining receipt (sMON, aprMON, gMON, and anything that could not be swapped) to the creator. The vault can stake into those protocols; it cannot unstake. Redeem the receipts in Kintsu, aPriori or Magma.
4. `withdraw` the vault’s MON to the creator. This does not pause the vault.
5. Optional: pause the agent afterwards (the “close” switch, on by default). A paused vault is resumed first if a swap or unwrap is needed, then paused again only when that switch is on.

Team vaults show the same report with the cash-out button disabled. The flow never calls `emergencyWithdrawAll`.
